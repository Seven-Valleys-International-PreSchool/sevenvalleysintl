Terms of Admission

Seven Valleys International Preschool

1. General Information

Seven Valleys International Preschool welcomes children into a caring, supportive, and engaging early-learning environment. These Terms of Admission explain the general conditions governing enquiries, registration, admission, attendance, fees, and the responsibilities of parents or guardians.

Submission of an enquiry or registration form does not automatically guarantee admission. Admission is subject to the school's eligibility criteria, availability of seats, verification of required documents, and confirmation by the school.

2. Eligibility and Age Criteria

Admission is offered to children who meet the age criteria prescribed for the relevant programme or class.

Parents are requested to provide the child's correct date of birth and any supporting documentation required by the school. Age eligibility and class placement will be determined in accordance with the school's admission guidelines and applicable educational requirements.

The school reserves the right to request additional information where clarification is necessary.

3. Registration and Admission Procedure

The admission process may include the following steps:

  • 1. Submission of an admission enquiry or registration form.
  • 2. Interaction with parents or guardians, where required.
  • 3. Submission and verification of the necessary documents.
  • 4. Confirmation of seat availability and applicable fees.
  • 5. Payment of the prescribed admission charges.
  • 6. Issuance of admission confirmation by the school.

Parents must ensure that all information provided during registration and admission is accurate and complete. Any material discrepancy or misrepresentation may result in a review of the admission.

4. Documents Required

Parents may be required to submit the following documents, as applicable:

  • Child's birth certificate or other accepted proof of age.
  • Recent photograph of the child.
  • Photographs or identification details of parents or guardians, where required.
  • Residential address and contact information.
  • Relevant medical information, including known allergies or special care requirements.
  • Transfer or previous-school documents, if applicable.
  • Any additional documents reasonably required under the school's admission procedure or applicable rules.

The school will handle submitted documents in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

5. Fees and Payment

The applicable admission fee, tuition fee, and other charges will be communicated to parents at the time of admission.

  • Fees must be paid according to the schedule communicated by the school.
  • Parents should retain payment receipts for their records.
  • Any applicable late-payment charges must be communicated in advance and applied in accordance with the school's approved fee policy and applicable law.
  • Additional charges for optional services, activities, or facilities will be communicated separately, where applicable.
  • Parents should contact the school office promptly if they have questions concerning a fee payment or account statement.

The school's fee structure and payment schedule may be revised from time to time, subject to applicable requirements and appropriate communication to parents.

6. Refunds and Admission Withdrawal

Requests for cancellation of admission or withdrawal from the school must be submitted in writing to the school office.

Refund eligibility, including any applicable deductions, will be determined according to the school's communicated refund policy, the timing of the request, the nature of the fees paid, and applicable law.

Any non-refundable fee or deduction must be clearly communicated to parents before payment and must be consistent with applicable requirements.

Parents are encouraged to obtain written acknowledgement of withdrawal requests and confirmation of any applicable refund.

7. Attendance and School Timings

Regular attendance supports continuity in a child's learning and development.

  • Parents should ensure that children arrive and are collected within the prescribed school timings.
  • Absences should be communicated to the school.
  • Parents should inform the school of any extended absence or circumstances affecting attendance.
  • Children who are unwell should remain at home in accordance with the school's health guidelines.
  • Repeated late arrival or collection may be discussed with parents to support the child's well-being and smooth school operations.

8. Health, Hygiene, and Medical Information

Parents must provide accurate information about relevant medical conditions, allergies, dietary restrictions, and other needs that may affect the child's safety or participation in school activities.

Parents should notify the school promptly of any material change in this information.

The school will take reasonable steps to support children's health and safety within its facilities and capabilities. In a medical emergency, the school may contact the parent or guardian and arrange appropriate medical assistance, including emergency services when necessary.

Parents remain responsible for providing accurate emergency contact details and informing the school of any changes.

9. Child Safety and Authorised Collection

Children will be released only to a parent, guardian, or other person authorised by the parent and accepted by the school, subject to applicable law and any relevant court order.

Parents must follow the school's visitor, arrival, departure, and pickup procedures. The school may verify the identity of an authorised person before releasing a child.

The school's Child Safety Policy forms an important part of its commitment to providing a secure and nurturing learning environment.

10. Behaviour and Parent Cooperation

The school promotes kindness, respect, inclusion, and positive behaviour appropriate to each child's developmental stage.

Parents and guardians are expected to:

  • Treat children, teachers, staff, and other families respectfully.
  • Communicate concerns through appropriate school channels.
  • Provide accurate information relevant to the child's care and education.
  • Cooperate with teachers and school management to support the child's development.
  • Follow reasonable campus safety and visitor procedures.

The school will address behavioural concerns through age-appropriate guidance and communication with parents, rather than corporal punishment or humiliating disciplinary practices.

11. Learning Activities and School Events

The school may organise classroom activities, celebrations, educational visits, and other age-appropriate programmes to support children's learning and development.

Parents will be informed of relevant arrangements and any necessary permissions or requirements for activities outside the usual school routine.

Participation in activities involving additional arrangements or costs may be subject to separate communication and applicable consent requirements.

12. Photographs, Videos, and Communication

The school may communicate with parents through telephone calls, messages, email, notices, or other official channels.

Photographs and videos may be captured during school activities. Any use of a child's image for public promotional purposes will be handled in accordance with the school's privacy policy and applicable consent requirements.

Parents should ensure that their contact information remains current so that important notices and emergency communications can be delivered effectively.

13. CCTV and Campus Security

CCTV may be used in designated areas of the campus for safety and security purposes, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Where parent access to live CCTV is provided, it will be governed by the school's access procedures. Such access must not be shared with unauthorised persons or used to record, reproduce, or distribute footage without permission.

14. School Policies and Updates

Parents are requested to familiarise themselves with the school's Child Safety Policy, Privacy Policy, fee policy, health guidelines, and other applicable school rules.

The school may update its procedures when necessary to improve educational services, safety, administration, or compliance with applicable law. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.

15. Admission Confirmation and Termination

Admission is considered confirmed only after the school has completed the prescribed formalities and communicated its acceptance.

The school may review an admission where material information was withheld or falsified, or where other serious circumstances arise. Any suspension, withdrawal, or termination of admission will be handled fairly, with appropriate communication to parents and in accordance with applicable law and the school's established procedures.

16. Acknowledgement

By submitting an admission application and accepting an offer of admission, parents or guardians acknowledge that they have been given an opportunity to review the school's admission terms and relevant policies.

Parents are encouraged to contact the school office for clarification before completing the admission process.
