Privacy Policy

Seven Valleys International Preschool

1. Introduction

Seven Valleys International Preschool respects the privacy of children, parents, guardians, staff members, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and handle personal information in connection with our website, admissions process, and school-related activities.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection and privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the services used and the stage of admission, the school may collect the following information:

A. Parent or Guardian Information

  • Name, contact number, email address, and residential address.
  • Relationship to the child.
  • Emergency contact details.
  • Information submitted through enquiry and admission forms..

B. Child Information

  • Child's name, date of birth, and photograph, where required.
  • Information necessary for admission and educational planning.
  • Relevant medical information, allergies, or special care requirements voluntarily provided or lawfully required.
  • Previous school details, where applicable.

C. Website Information

  • Information submitted through contact forms, admission enquiries, or other website forms.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, where collected through server logs or analytics tools.

The school aims to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

3. How We Use Personal Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to parent enquiries and admission requests.
  • Processing registrations, admissions, and school records.
  • Communicating school notices, events, activities, and important updates.
  • Contacting parents or guardians during emergencies.
  • Supporting children's health, safety, care, and educational needs.
  • Managing fee records and other administrative activities.
  • Maintaining website security and improving website performance.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, and child-protection obligations.

Marketing communications, photographs used for promotional purposes, and other optional uses will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and the permissions or consents needed for the particular use.

4. Children's Personal Information

We recognise that children's personal information requires particular care.

Where consent or authorisation is required for collecting or processing a child's information, the school will seek it from the parent or lawful guardian in accordance with applicable law.

The school will take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, misuse, or loss of children's information. Access to child-related records will be limited to authorised persons who need the information for legitimate school purposes.

5. Photographs, Videos, and School Activities

Photographs or videos may be taken during classroom activities, celebrations, events, and other school programmes.

Where images or recordings are intended for public promotional use, such as the school website, social media pages, brochures, or advertisements, the school will obtain the necessary permissions or consents as required by applicable law and school procedures.

Parents who have concerns about the use of their child's photographs should contact the school management.

6. Sharing of Information

The school does not intend to sell children's personal information or parent contact details.

Information may be shared, where necessary and lawful, with:

  • Authorised school staff and management.
  • Service providers supporting school operations, website hosting, communication, or administration.
  • Healthcare providers or emergency services when required for a child's safety or medical care.
  • Government authorities, law-enforcement agencies, or other parties when disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect a child's vital interests.

Where third-party service providers process personal information on the school's behalf, appropriate safeguards will be applied as required.

7. Data Security and Retention

The school takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, educational, and record-retention requirements.

No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The school will take appropriate steps to address suspected data breaches in accordance with applicable law.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

The website may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, and performance. Analytics or other non-essential technologies may also be used if implemented.

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate information and choices regarding cookies and similar technologies.

9. Parent Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, parents or authorised guardians may contact the school to request access to, correction of, or other appropriate action concerning personal information held about themselves or their child.

The school may need to verify the identity and authority of the person making a request before responding.

10. External Links

The school website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. The school is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in school practices, technology, or applicable law. The latest version will be published on this website with its updated effective date, where appropriate.
