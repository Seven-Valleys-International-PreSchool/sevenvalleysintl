Child Safety Policy

Seven Valleys International Preschool is a early learning space where children feel safe and confident.

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

At Seven Valleys International Preschool, the safety, security, dignity, and well-being of every child are our highest priorities. We are committed to providing a nurturing, inclusive, and child-friendly environment where children feel safe, respected, supported, and encouraged to learn.

We believe every child has the right to protection from abuse, neglect, discrimination, bullying, and any form of physical or emotional harm. All staff members, caregivers, and associated personnel are expected to uphold these principles.

2. Safe Campus and Supervision

  • Children are supervised by designated staff during classroom activities, outdoor play, transitions, and school events.
  • Age-appropriate classroom furniture, toys, and learning materials are selected to support children's safety.
  • Classrooms, play areas, washrooms, and common spaces are maintained with appropriate cleanliness and safety measures.
  • Visitors are required to follow the school's entry and identification procedures.
  • Children must not be left unattended in classrooms, play areas, vehicles, or other school premises.
  • Outdoor play and physical activities are conducted with age-appropriate supervision.

3. Child Arrival and Authorised Pickup

  • Parents or guardians should ensure that children arrive at school within the prescribed timings.
  • Children will be released only to a parent, guardian, or other person authorised by the parent and accepted by the school.
  • The school may request identity verification before releasing a child.
  • Parents must inform the school promptly of any changes to authorised pickup arrangements.
  • Any delay in collecting a child must be communicated to the school as early as possible.
  • In the event of a custody-related restriction or court order, parents must provide the relevant documentation to the school.

4. Staff Conduct and Child Protection

All staff members are expected to maintain professional boundaries and treat every child with patience, kindness, and respect.

The school does not permit:

  • Corporal punishment or physical intimidation.
  • Verbal abuse, humiliation, threats, or degrading language.
  • Bullying, discrimination, or deliberate exclusion.
  • Inappropriate physical contact or conduct that compromises a child's dignity.
  • Any form of abuse, exploitation, harassment, or neglect.

Concerns about a child's safety or the conduct of any staff member must be reported promptly to the school management. All concerns will be handled seriously and in accordance with applicable child-protection laws and procedures.

5. Health, Hygiene, and Medical Emergencies

  • The school maintains appropriate hygiene and cleaning practices for classrooms, toys, and frequently touched surfaces.
  • Parents must share relevant emergency contact details and essential medical information with the school.
  • Parents should inform the school about allergies, ongoing medical needs, or other conditions requiring special attention.
  • Children who are unwell should remain at home until they are fit to attend, following the school's illness guidelines.
  • In a medical emergency, the school will contact the parent or guardian and arrange appropriate medical assistance, including emergency services when necessary.
  • Any medication administered at school must follow the school's approved medication procedure and applicable requirements.

6. CCTV and Campus Security

CCTV surveillance may be used in designated areas of the campus to support safety and security, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Where live CCTV access is offered to parents, it will be provided only through the school's approved access arrangements. Access credentials must not be shared or misused. CCTV will not be installed in areas where privacy is reasonably expected, such as washrooms.

CCTV footage will be accessed, retained, and disclosed only by authorised personnel for legitimate purposes and in accordance with applicable law and school procedures.

7. Child-Friendly and Respectful Environment

We encourage children to communicate their feelings, ask questions, seek help, and express concerns without fear. Our educators aim to support positive behaviour through encouragement, guidance, patience, and age-appropriate communication.

The school promotes respect for individual differences and aims to ensure that every child is treated fairly and with dignity.

8. Parent Partnership and Reporting Concerns

Parents and guardians are important partners in maintaining a safe learning environment. Any safety concern, injury, suspected misconduct, or safeguarding issue should be reported to the Principal or designated school authority.

The school will review concerns promptly, maintain appropriate confidentiality, take suitable protective action, and refer matters to the relevant authorities where required by law.

9. Policy Review

This policy may be reviewed and updated periodically to reflect improvements in school practices, child-safety standards, and applicable legal requirements.

For child-safety concerns, please contact the school office or Principal through the official contact details published on this website.
