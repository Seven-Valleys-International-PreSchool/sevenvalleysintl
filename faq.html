Ask Our Team →
What age groups do you accept? +

We welcome young children across our early-years programs. The right program is based on your child's age, developmental stage and readiness. Please contact our admissions team for current age criteria and availability.

What preschool programs do you offer? +

Our preschool experience focuses on joyful, play-based learning. Children build early language, numeracy, motor, social and communication skills through stories, songs, creative activities, movement and hands-on exploration.

Do you offer day care along with preschool? +

Yes. Our day care is designed to provide a warm, engaging environment beyond regular preschool hours, with age-appropriate activities, routines, play and quiet time.

What does a typical day at preschool look like? +

A typical day includes arrival and free play, circle time, guided learning experiences, creative activities, outdoor or movement play, meals or snacks, rest or quiet time and opportunities for social interaction.

How do you support learning through play? +

Children learn through purposeful play, hands-on experiences and exploration. Teachers use stories, puzzles, music, art, sensory activities, movement and everyday problem-solving to make learning meaningful and enjoyable.

How do you keep children safe and secure? +

Child safety is part of our everyday routines. We use supervised spaces, clear arrival and departure procedures, age-appropriate environments and attentive adult supervision throughout the school and day care experience.

How do you help a child settle into preschool? +

We aim to make the transition gentle and reassuring. Teachers build familiar routines, positive relationships and a sense of belonging while working with parents to understand each child's personality and comfort needs.

Do you provide meals and snacks? +

Meal and snack arrangements depend on the current program and school policy. Please speak with our team about the available options, timings and how dietary requirements or allergies are handled.

What activities do children enjoy at Seven Valleys? +

Children enjoy a variety of activities including music, learning games, painting and creative work, sports, movement and playground time. These experiences support confidence, coordination, communication, creativity and social development.

How do you support a child's individual needs? +

Teachers observe children closely and adapt activities, communication and routines to their interests, strengths and developmental needs. We also value regular communication with families.

What are the day care timings? +

Our listed school hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Day care schedules and availability can vary, so please contact the school to confirm the current timings for your child's program.

Can parents visit the school before admission? +

Absolutely. We encourage families to visit the campus, see the learning environment, meet our team and discuss their child's needs before making an admission decision.

How do I apply for admission? +

Start by contacting us or booking a campus visit. Our team can explain the available programs, current openings, required documents, fees and the next steps in the admission process.

How do teachers communicate with parents? +

We believe in an open partnership with families. Parents can discuss their child's progress, routines, experiences and questions with the school team through the communication channels provided by the school.

What should my child bring to school or day care? +

The exact list depends on the child's program. Typically, families may need comfortable clothing, a labelled water bottle and any program-specific personal items requested by the school. Our team will provide the current checklist during admission.
