⭐ Our academic approach

Learning that feels like play.

Children learn best when they are curious, active and emotionally secure.

At Seven Valleys, lessons are designed around meaningful experiences. We combine language, numeracy, creativity, movement, social-emotional development and sensory exploration so children build strong foundations while enjoying every step of learning.

Every lesson supports more than academics — we nurture confidence, independence, communication and a love of discovery.

How We Teach

Six pillars behind every lesson plan

Language & phonics

Story-led vocabulary building and early reading skills.

Numeracy & logic

Hands-on counting, patterns and early problem-solving.

Creative arts

Painting, music and craft that build fine motor skills.

Physical development

Outdoor play and structured movement for gross motor growth.

Social-emotional skills

Sharing, turn-taking and naming feelings through group play.

Sensory exploration

Sand, water and texture play that fuels curiosity daily.

Programmes

Explore each age group

A home away from home

Our youngest learners get gentle routines, sensory play and lots of cuddles — designed to ease separation and build trust.

  • 1:5 caregiver ratio with dedicated nap room
  • Sensory bins, soft blocks and music time
  • Daily parent updates on feeding & nap

First friendships, first stories

Circle-time songs, picture books and guided group play help children build language and their very first friendships.

  • Story & music circle every morning
  • Colour, shape & sorting activities
  • Outdoor play deck time daily

Curiosity takes the lead

Phonics, early numeracy and hands-on projects turn everyday questions into structured discovery.

  • Letter sounds & pre-writing practice
  • Counting, patterns & simple sorting
  • Weekly themed project & show-and-tell

Building blocks for reading & math

Structured phonics blends, number operations and guided writing prepare children for confident, independent learning.

  • Blended phonics & sight-word reading
  • Number operations up to 20
  • Guided handwriting practice
