Admissions Open: Give Your Child the Best Start

We maintain a strict 1:4 teacher-child ratio. Because seats are limited to preserve individual nurturing, early registration is highly encouraged.

Transparent & Simple

The 4-Step Admission Journey

No formal entrance tests or stressful interviews for toddlers. Just pure joy and understanding.

01

Campus Tour & Counseling

Visit our green campus, inspect the live CCTV infrastructure, experience our Montessori apparatus, and discuss your child's milestones with our principal.

02

Playful Child Interaction

An informal 20-minute fun play session in our sensory room. We observe social comfort, eye contact, and motor agility to customize their classroom integration.

03

Simple Documentation

Submit the child's birth certificate, immunization chart, and guardian ID proofs. Confirmation is granted within 24 hours of seat availability.

04

Welcome Kit & Orientation

Receive the official Seven Valleys Welcome Kit (backpack, Montessori learning journals, uniform, parent app login) and attend our warm parent orientation.
