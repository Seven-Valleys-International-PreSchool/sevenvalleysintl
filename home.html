Parents, we've got you

Questions?
Let's make it easy.

Here are answers to some of the questions families ask us most often.

Ask us anything →
What age groups do you accept?+

We welcome children from around 2 years through kindergarten age. Exact program placement is based on age and readiness.

Do you offer full-day day care?+

Yes. Our day care program is designed for families who need a safe, engaging environment before and after preschool hours.

What does a typical day look like?+

Children enjoy a balanced rhythm of arrival, free play, circle time, guided activities, outdoor play, meals and quiet time.

How do you keep children safe?+

We use supervised entry and exit, age-appropriate spaces, trained staff and clear child-safety routines throughout the day.

Do you provide meals and snacks?+

Meal arrangements can be tailored to the program. Please speak with our admissions team about the current menu and allergy procedures.

How do I apply for admission?+

Send us an enquiry, book a campus visit and our admissions team will guide you through availability, documentation and next steps.

Can parents visit the school before enrolling?+

Absolutely. We encourage families to visit, meet the team and see the learning spaces before making a decision.

How do you support each child's individual needs?+

Teachers observe each child closely and adapt activities, communication and routines to support different interests, strengths and developmental needs.

Do you offer a settling-in period?+

Yes. We work with families to make the transition gentle, predictable and reassuring for both child and parent.
